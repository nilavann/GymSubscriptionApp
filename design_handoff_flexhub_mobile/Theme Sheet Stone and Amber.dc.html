Fit & Fine Gym · Theme sheet

Stone & Amber

A warm-neutral management theme. Stone carries the interface; amber is the only saturated colour and marks one thing — something a staff member can act on. The orange logo sits on top of the system as a brand mark rather than driving the interface, which is why the neutrals are warm instead of grey.

01 · Tier one

Primitives

Raw stops. These are never referenced directly in a component — they exist so the semantic layer has somewhere to point. Both ramps run the full ten stops: stone supplies the structure, and amber needs its own range because the light-mode roles sit at 700–800 while their dark-mode counterparts sit at 300–600.
